[B]Development note[/B]
This addon was developed with AI assistance. The author reviews and maintains the code.

[B]What it does[/B]
In-game companion for MacMinion. After you log in it writes your roster, which addons actually loaded, and compact SavedVariables / LibAddonMenu layouts to its own SavedVariables file. MacMinion reads that file when ESO is closed. Nothing is sent anywhere.
